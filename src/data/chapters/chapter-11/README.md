# Chapter 11 Dossier: Operational Handover (From Project Delivery to Operational Ownership)

## 📌 Executive Summary
Following the v1.0.0 documentation and release milestone in Chapter 10, the project faces its ultimate sustainability challenge. The business is satisfied and the deployment looks stable, but Marcus (Head of IT Operations) refuses to accept ownership simply because the delivery team is ready to move on.

As Project Manager, you must prove that the receiving operations team can independently operate, troubleshoot, and maintain Project Amigo at 2 AM without relying on the developers who originally built it.

---

## 🎯 Transition Governance Objectives
1. **Operational Readiness (Handover Package)**: Match 6 core requirements to administrator runbooks, escalation matrices, access guides, monitoring dashboards, defect registers, and maintenance SOPs.
2. **Knowledge Transfer & Live Incident Drill**: Validate an incident response sequence for ticket `INC-0318` (Employee login failures) and schedule 3 mandatory recorded workshops.
3. **14-Day Hypercare Window**: Structure a progressive support model across Intensive Monitoring (Days 1–2), Shared Ownership (Days 3–7), and Operations-Led Support (Days 8–14).
4. **Operational Acceptance Sign-Off**: Secure formal signed acceptance from Marcus on behalf of IT Operations.

---

## 👥 Key Stakeholders
- **Marcus (Head of IT Operations)**: Demanding operational runbooks, incident validation, and hypercare support before accepting ownership.
- **Aarav Kapoor (Senior PM · Mentor)**: Mentoring the transition from project delivery into genuine operational ownership.
- **Daniel (Lead Developer)**: Ensuring technical knowledge is transferred so engineering can transition to upcoming feature cycles.
- **Martha (Junior PM · Transition Coordination)**: Scheduling knowledge transfer workshops and updating the transition calendar.
