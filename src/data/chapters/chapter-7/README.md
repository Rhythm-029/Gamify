# Chapter 7 Dossier: The Resistance (Change Management Crisis)

## 📌 Executive Summary
Technology rarely fails transformations; culture and fear do. 80,000 branch employees feel threatened by automated appraisal metrics. Union leadership threatens work-to-rule protests.

## 🎯 Strategic Actions
- Meeting with union delegates to demonstrate that performance analytics are formative, not punitive.
- Replacing 4-hour mandatory slide lectures with 5-minute interactive micro-learning modules on employee mobile phones.
- Establishing an internal Employee Advisory Council.
