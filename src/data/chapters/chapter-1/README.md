# Chapter 1 Dossier: The Briefing (Project Amigo Kickoff)

## 📌 Executive Summary
As a newly embedded Digital Transformation Consultant at Titan Manufacturing representing Brained, you participate in the high-stakes Project Amigo kickoff meeting. ICICI Bank requires an enterprise HRMS solution for 80,000 employees with 12 modules in 90 days.

## 👥 Key Stakeholders
- **Marcus Reed (CTO · Brained)**: Demands technical feasibility, capacity testing (30,000 peak logins), and architectural sanity.
- **Sophia Bennett (Client Lead · ICICI Bank)**: Demands all 12 modules in 90 days to meet the non-negotiable nationwide appraisal cycle.
- **Daniel Brooks (Program Manager · Brained)**: Flags the blocking absence of LDAP sandbox credentials for SSO.
- **Aarav Kapoor (Senior Consultant · Brained)**: Assigns the player with correcting ReadMe AI minutes and scoping the initial delivery phase.

## 🎯 Chapter Flow
1. **Camera & Presence Check**: Join kickoff meeting with camera on (+10 Trust, +20 XP).
2. **Kickoff Meeting Part 1**: Marcus introduces Project Amigo; Sophia issues 90-day mandate; Marcus questions feasibility.
3. **MCQ Critical Governance Decision**:
   - Option A: Agree to 90 days (-10 Risk penalty).
   - Option B: Outright rejection (-5 Stakeholder friction).
   - Option C (Ideal): Scope, capacity, and dependency validation (+25 Governance).
4. **Kickoff Meeting Part 2**: Marcus mentions 30,000 concurrent login challenge; Daniel flags LDAP credentials; Aarav directs player to ReadMe AI.
5. **ReadMe AI MoM Verification**: Player inspects and fixes AI hallucinated minutes, submitting the official record.
6. **Teams Audio Briefing & Document**: Teams pops open with message chime and Aarav's message attaching `Project_Amigo_Initial_Scope_Planning.pdf`.
7. **Scope Planning Assignment 01**:
   - Task 1: Prioritize Appraisals, Attendance, and Leave.
   - Task 2: Record LDAP as an explicit dependency and proceed with non-blocked tracks.
   - Task 3: Mandate 30,000 load testing and core data flows as gates.
8. **Evaluation & Chapter Completion**: Aarav provides feedback; player unlocks Chapter 2 in the session!
