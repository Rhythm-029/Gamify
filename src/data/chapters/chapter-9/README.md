# Chapter 9 Dossier: ROI Validation (Executive Leadership Phase)

## 📌 Executive Summary
Project Amigo is live in production across 80,000 ICICI Bank employees. Martha compiles verified APM telemetry showing an astounding **42% reduction in appraisal cycle completion time** (from 21.0 days down to 12.2 days) and **15,000 verified operational man-hours saved** across Mumbai, Pune, Bangalore, and Delhi pilot branches. 

However, technical delivery and uptime are not enough to guarantee continuation. In the Executive Boardroom, **CFO Vikram Malhotra** (who demanded tangible ROI within 60 days in Chapter 2) scrutinizes whether saved hours produce real balance sheet cash-flow and EBITDA growth, while **CTO Marcus Reed** (from Chapter 1 & Chapter 6) challenges whether the architecture can scale to all 4,800 nationwide branches without repeating Chapter 6 database connection pool saturation or Chapter 7 attendance sync 504 memory leaks.
